## Public holidays (examples in calendar)

- National Day: 1 October 2025
- The day following Mid-Autumn Festival: 7 October 2025
- Chung Yeung Festival: 29 October 2025

## Make-up examinations

Apply within **5 working days** after a missed exam. Check AR notices for exact deadlines each term.
