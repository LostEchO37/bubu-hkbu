# HKBU Campus Life & Student Services (Summary)

Sources: Student Handbook 2025–2026, Office of Student Affairs, HKBU website.

## General

HKBU provides accommodation, Learning Commons, library, catering, sports, career services, counselling, and student activities on Kowloon Tong campus and related facilities.

- Campus life overview: https://www.hkbu.edu.hk/en/campus-life.html
- Student handbook (student life): https://handbook.ar.hkbu.edu.hk/2025-2026/student-life

## Office of Student Affairs (SA)

SA supports whole-person development: student organisations, accommodation, career advising, counselling, leadership and service-learning, lockers, and campus amenities.

## Counselling (FAQ highlights)

- Free for eligible full-time and UGC-funded part-time students.
- Sessions typically 50–60 minutes.
- Confidential; not shown on academic transcript.
- Appointment: visit the Centre or call **3411 7435** during service hours.

## Accommodation

- **Village CARE** at Jockey Club Campus of Creativity — residential education, mixed UG/PG/exchange.
- **Undergraduate Halls** — traditional hall life (~1,770 residents).
- Details: Student Handbook → Student Accommodation Section.

## Learning & study spaces

- **Library**: https://library.hkbu.edu.hk
- **Learning Commons**: collaborative study space managed under Campus Life & Amenities (CLA).
