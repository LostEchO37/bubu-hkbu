1. CS ACCOUNT ACTIVATION
================================================================================
Computer Science Department accounts:
You have one set of accounts for using Departmental computing systems - Windows and Linux systems (CSDLABAD.LOCAL domain), Mac, UNIX, and email systems.

For each new student, you will receive an email about how to get the account information. Please follow the instructions to complete your account activation.

IMPORTANT: You should activate your CS account BEFORE you can gain access to all computer facilities and services of the department.

Sample account information:
- Student Name: David Wong
- Student ID number: 23012345
- Username: f3012345
- Password: (a random number provided initially)

David can use this account to log on to Windows & Linux systems under the CSDLABAD.LOCAL domain within CS computer labs (COMP labs), to log on to UNIX hosts (e.g. elpis, or faith), to check his email, and to submit assignments.

David should change his account password after the first login to the systems.

SSOid (BUniPort / ITO):
Single Sign On user id (SSOid) is another account provided by ITO for BUniPort and central IT services. The SSOid for a student is pre-defined to be the student number. Students must register/activate SSOid before gaining access to central IT resources. See ITO quick guide: https://ito.hkbu.edu.hk/services/quick-guide-for-new-students.html
Contact ITO: 3411-7899 or hotline@hkbu.edu.hk

2. HOW TO CHANGE CS ACCOUNT PASSWORD
================================================================================
To change the password for Windows, CSLinux, Unix, email and Mac Systems:
1. Go to https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/setpass
2. Key in your Login Name, Old Password, New Password, Re-type Password, and Captcha
3. Click the Proceed button
4. Then read the on-screen NOTICES to complete your password-change process.

4. OVERNIGHT STAY IN CS LABS
================================================================================
Students are NOT allowed to stay overnight in CS Labs without prior approval.

Procedure for Overnight Stay Application in CS Computer Labs:
1. Download two forms via Intranet -> Forms Download Center -> Forms for students -> Application for Overnight Stay in CS Computer Labs:
   a) Application for Overnight Stay in Computer Laboratory of Computer Science Department
   b) Application for Extension of Stay on Campus (three copies required by Estates Office)
2. Complete Form a and Form b; Form a should be signed by the individual student.
3. Hand in Form a and Form b to the course teacher for approval.
4. If endorsed, submit them to General Office (RRS702) for further approval.
5. Get Form b back from General Office (RRS702). Take it to the Security Office at RRS building Level 2, at least 24 hours in advance, to obtain Estate Office approval.
6. Submit the endorsed Form b to Technical Support Team offices (RRS632 or FSC901B) for updating lab door access permission.

Even if an assignment deadline is approaching, overnight stay is only allowed after completing this approval procedure.

5. PRINT QUOTA CHECKING
================================================================================
How to check my print quota?

Method 1:
Visit http://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/pquota/ with your COMP Unix account (same as your email account).

Method 2:
1. Login a Unix host (e.g. faith.comp.hkbu.edu.hk) with your COMP Unix account (same as your email account).
2. At the Login prompt type: pqouta -v
   Example output: 000 Quota for UID 19607 (d9012345) is 60 (soft=60, perm=0)

Print quota policy (CS students, provided on 1st September each year):
- Year 1: Yearly 400 pages
- Year 2: Yearly 500 pages
- Year 3: Yearly 600 pages
- MSc: Yearly 600 pages

At the end of each academic year, the print quota balance will be carried forward. Remaining print quota expires on 31 August of graduation year.
Students may extend print quota at $30 per 100 sheets at General Office (RRS702).
